Research

Multimodality ophthalmic imaging technologies

We introduce spectrally encoded coherence tomography and reflectometry (SECTR), a high-speed multimodality system for simultaneous OCT and spectrally-encoded reflectance (SER) imaging at 2 gigapixels-per-second.

Collaborators: Karen Joos, M.D., Ph.D., Shriji Patel, M.D., Anthony Daniels, M.D., Irina De la Huerta, M.D., Ph.D.

Intraoperative imaging for ophthalmic surgical guidance

We have developed multimodal SECTR technologies for simultaneous and intrinsically co-registered en face SER and cross-sectional OCT imaging. We recently demonstrated an intraoperative SECTR (iSECTR) microscope-integrated scan-head for video-rate volumetric (4D) imaging at 18 volumes-per-second.

Collaborators: Karen Joos, M.D., Ph.D., Shriji Patel, M.D., Christine Shieh, M.D.

Machine-learning assisted imaging of surgical dynamics

We demonstrate automated surgical instrument-tracking and adaptive-sampling of OCT using a combination of deep-learning and SECTR. We believe this method overcomes critical barriers to clinical translation of iOCT.

Collaborators: Karen Joos, M.D., Ph.D., Shriji Patel, M.D., Christine Shieh, M.D.

Point-of-care ophthalmic diagnostic imaging

We describe a handheld SECTR system that enables simultaneous en face reflectance and cross-sectional OCT imaging with a compact optomechanical design for bedside and pediatric patients.

Collaborators: Ipek Oguz, Ph.D., Anthony Daniels, M.D., Irina De la Huerta, M.D., Ph.D.

Structural and functional imaging in rodent models

Rodent models are robust tools for understanding human retinal disease and function. OCT enables depth-resolved visualization of structures and image-based surrogate biomarkers of disease.

Collaborators: Edward Levine, Ph.D.

Quantitative vascular imaging

We present a novel method for quantitative measurements of lung vasculature using multi-volumetric optical coherence microscopy (OCM) in optically cleared murine lungs.

Collaborators: Susan Majka, Ph.D.
